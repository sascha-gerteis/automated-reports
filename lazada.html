Main Problems

Revenue Concentration

Over 95% of revenue is from a single product (Columbia Pictures Aquaverse daily ticket).

Reason: Lack of product diversification, insufficient marketing/optimization for other products.

Overall Scale vs. Top Stores

Significantly lower revenue, orders, and buyers compared to top stores in the same category.

Reason: Lower traffic volume, less effective overall marketing, narrower product catalog, or competitive pricing disadvantages (despite higher AOV).

Ad Spend Efficiency

Ad spend is allocated to irrelevant keywords (e.g., 'swensens', 'grab voucher', 'popmart') that generate zero revenue.

Reason: Poor keyword targeting or broad match settings in advertising campaigns.

Product Conversion

Many products receive traffic and even 'add to cart' actions but generate no revenue.

Reason: Unclear product descriptions, high pricing, poor imagery, lack of compelling offers, or poor user experience on product pages.

Chat Support

5 customers were not responded to via chat, resulting in an estimated lost revenue of 2,821.20.

Reason: Potential staffing shortages or missed notifications during busy periods.
