#19 ★★★★☆ 2026-02-07 Charis Young 
We recommend booking via klook, they do buy one get one free so cost us 1590 baht for both of us. There are some great rides here, even for adults, plenty of sun loungers too. We went on a Saturday and we walked on to most of the rides without a queue. Locker hire is 300 baht and you can rent a towel for 200 baht (refundable). The downside for us is you cannot take any food or drink inside, even water bottles so you have to pay expensive prices for these. Water is only 30 baht but a burger without fries is around 600 baht! Overall, it was a great day and we spent around 6 hours here, just be mindful of these prices.
