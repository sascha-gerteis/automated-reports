Recommended actions
  • **Proactively Share Key Information:** A high volume of neutral comments in both periods relate to basic operational questions (prices, opening hours, location, ticket validity, specific ride access, rental availability). The social team should create more prominent and easily accessible posts (e.g., pinned posts, FAQs, dedicated story highlights) that answer these common inquiries to reduce repetitive questions.
  • **Highlight Positive Staff Interactions & Review Staff Training:** Leverage the strong positive feedback about 'helpful, kind, and remarkably efficient' staff (ID: 7, current period) by creating content that features team members. Simultaneously, review and reinforce customer service training, especially for areas like the wave pool, to prevent recurrence of rude staff complaints seen in the previous period (ID: 22, 23).
  • **Encourage Deeper Engagement for Neutral Comments:** Many neutral comments are simple tags or expressions of interest. The social team can increase engagement by responding to these comments with open-ended questions (e.g., 'What are you most excited to try?', 'Which friend would you bring to experience this?'). For questions that aren't immediately answered by FAQs, direct them to a specific contact method or provide a concise answer.

✅ What People Love

  • Overall Enjoyment & Entertainment — Visitors consistently reported having a great, fun, and enjoyable time at the water park, suitable for family entertainment.
  • Cleanliness and Maintenance — The park is described as very clean, well-maintained, and modern, contributing to a pleasant and organized atmosphere.
  • Quality and Variety of Slides — The slides and rides are often highlighted as being amazing, good quality, and offering a variety of exciting options for all.
  • Friendly and Attentive Staff — Many guests found the staff to be polite, friendly, and attentive, enhancing their overall experience.
  • Online Ticket Discounts — The availability of cheaper tickets through online booking platforms is a positive aspect, allowing visitors to save money on entry.

❌ What Needs Improvement

  • High Prices for Food and Drinks (3 evidence) — Many guests expressed dissatisfaction with the expensive cost of food and beverages inside the park, considering them overpriced.
  • High Ticket Prices and Extra Costs (3 evidence) — The entry tickets are considered expensive, especially when purchased at the gate, with some visitors feeling that "everything is extra money."
  • Physical Exertion from Climbing Stairs (3 evidence) — Guests frequently mentioned the significant physical effort required to climb many flights of stairs while carrying tubes to access the slides.
  • Food and Drink Restrictions (3 evidence) — Visitors are generally not allowed to bring their own food or water into the park, forcing them to purchase potentially overpriced items inside.
  • Unprofessional or Unhelpful Staff (2 evidence) — Some reviews pointed out instances of unprofessional or unhelpful staff behavior, particularly concerning locker assistance or clarity on raft locations.
  • Outdated Facilities and Ride Experience Issues (3 evidence) — A few guests noted that some facilities felt outdated, or specific ride conditions (like hot, dark tubes or extremely hot floors) detracted from the experience.

💡 Opportunities & Trends

  • Quality of Slides & Rides — The water park's slides and rides are often praised for being amazing, fun, and of good quality, offering a variety of experiences.
  • Physical Exertion (Stairs) — Several guests commented on the considerable physical effort required to climb numerous stairs, often while carrying tubes, to reach the top of the slides.
