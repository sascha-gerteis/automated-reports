✅ What People Love

  • Overall Positive Experience & Park Quality — Many visitors had a great time, describing the park as excellent, fun, clean, and a wonderful place for family relaxation and activities.
  • Staff Friendliness & Helpfulness — Reviewers frequently highlighted positive interactions with staff, noting their attentiveness, politeness, and helpfulness, including one instance of honesty regarding a lost item.
  • Variety of Slides & Activities — The park was praised for its diverse range of slides, including options for both children and extreme thrill-seekers, as well as other attractions like shows and karting.
  • Low Crowds & Short Wait Times — Several guests enjoyed the park more due to minimal crowds and little to no waiting time for slides, particularly when visiting on weekdays or during the low season.
  • Shows & Entertainment — The shows, actors, and parades were specifically commended as excellent and a highlight of the water park experience.

❌ What Needs Improvement

  • High Prices (Food, Entry, Lockers) (3 evidence) — A common point of dissatisfaction was the expensive cost of food and drinks inside the park, along with high entrance fees and costly locker rentals.
  • Park Condition & Maintenance (3 evidence) — Guests reported that the park appears old, with worn-out and painful walkways, peeling surfaces on slides and pool floors, suggesting a decline in maintenance.
  • Issues with Slide Experience (3 evidence) — Problems encountered on slides included water hitting the face painfully, causing difficulty breathing and opening eyes, physically demanding stairs, slides getting stuck, and too much chlorine causing eye irritation.
  • Staff & Organization Issues (3 evidence) — Negative comments included unorganized staff, rude service, poor follow-up in food zones leading to cold food, and a lack of staff at some children's rides.
  • Unavailable / Restricted Attractions (1 evidence) — Visitors were disappointed when major attractions, such as the wave pool, were not fully operational without prior notification, despite paying the full admission price.

💡 Opportunities & Trends

  • Variety of Attractions — Guests appreciated the wide selection of slides, rides, children's areas, and additional entertainment like shows and other activities.
  • Park Condition & Maintenance — Several reviews mentioned the park showing its age, with worn-out walkways, peeling surfaces on slides and pools, and a general impression of declining maintenance.
