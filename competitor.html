Guests frequently highlight the park's cleanliness, the friendly and attentive staff, and its appeal for families and children, noting the reasonable prices and good food. The primary area for improvement identified is the inadequacy of the changing and locker room facilities, specifically the absence of seating and baby changing amenities.

Top positive themes

  • Cleanliness of the park
  • Friendly and attentive staff
  • Good for families and children
  • Not crowded/short queues

  • Great food

Top negative themes

  • Locker/changing room facilities (lack of seating, baby changing)

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Design and prominently feature superior changing and locker room facilities, including comfortable seating and dedicated baby changing stations, to directly address a competitor's weakness.
  • Highlight specific family-centric conveniences for parents with infants or young children to differentiate from the competitor's gaps.

  • Reinforce Aquaverse's commitment to cleanliness and attentive staff, as these are strong positive points for Ramayana that customers clearly value.

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket is highly praised for being a very nice and huge waterpark with lots of new rides, offering an amazing day out for families where kids have a blast. Customers frequently highlight the meticulous management and exceptional safety measures, noting the abundance of lifeguards. However, a significant negative point involves issues with purchasing tickets through their app, where advertised add-ons were reportedly fake, leading to double payments and advice to buy from local offices. Extra charges for locker and towel rentals are also mentioned. Andamanda Phuket received an excellent rating from its sole recent review, highlighting a very fun experience and commendable staff service. The reviewer expressed a clear intention to revisit with children, emphasizing the park's strong appeal as a family-friendly destination.

Top positive themes

  • Safety measures and lifeguards
  • Family-friendly experience
  • Variety of new rides
  • Fun
  • Good service
  • Family-friendly

Top negative themes

  • Misleading app-based ticket purchases
  • Extra charges for add-ons/rentals

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Ensure transparent and reliable online booking and app experience, avoiding hidden costs or misleading add-ons.
  • Clearly communicate all potential extra costs (e.g., lockers, towels) upfront or consider inclusive package deals.
  • Emphasize unique and diverse ride offerings to compete with the perception of 'lots of new rides'.
  • Highlight robust safety protocols and visible lifeguard presence to match or exceed competitor's high standards.
  • Ensure consistently high levels of staff service.
