Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket is highly praised for its friendly, attentive staff, exciting water slides, and overall cleanliness, offering a fun experience for families with short wait times. However, frequent complaints include the high cost of food and drinks, strict no outside food/water policies, and a perceived lack of value for money given the entry price. Guests also highlighted security concerns related to personal belongings and the sharp ground surface requiring additional shoe purchases. Andamanda Phuket is highly praised for its family-friendly atmosphere, extensive safety measures with numerous lifeguards, and the overall size and variety of activities. Customers appreciate the seamless entry process and often find good value when booking entry tickets through the Klook app. However, a significant point of dissatisfaction stems from issues with app bookings advertising fake add-ons that require additional payment, and the perceived high cost of locker and towel rentals. Based on one recent review, Andamanda Phuket received a perfect 5-star rating. The customer praised the park for being very fun and highlighted the staff for their good service. They expressed a strong intention to return with children.
