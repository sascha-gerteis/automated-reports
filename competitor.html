Customer voice summary

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is praised for its fun and thrilling water rides, family-friendly environment, and attentive staff. However, the overall customer experience is significantly marred by overwhelmingly high prices for everything within the park, exacerbated by a strict no-outside-food-or-water policy. Guests also express frustration over the physical exertion required to carry tubes up slides and raise concerns about maintenance and, critically, water quality.

Top positive themes

  • Thrilling & fun water rides
  • Family-friendly attractions & zones
  • Attentive & professional staff
  • Engaging shows & atmosphere

Top negative themes

  • Excessively high prices (tickets, food, drinks, lockers)
  • Strict no outside food/water policy
  • Tiring need to carry tubes up stairs
  • Concerns about water quality & maintenance
  • Limited variety of activities for some

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Review and adjust overall pricing strategy for tickets, food, and amenities.
  • Re-evaluate outside food and beverage policy, possibly offering free drinking water or more affordable options.
  • Implement solutions for carrying tubes up slides to improve guest comfort and experience.
  • Address maintenance concerns and conduct thorough checks on water quality.
  • Explore options to diversify activities or provide more value for the entry price.

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket receives high praise for its fun, thrilling, and well-maintained water slides, along with the friendly and safety-focused staff. Many visitors enjoy the family-friendly atmosphere and the generally low wait times, especially during off-peak periods. However, a significant drawback for guests is the perception of being overly 'money-focused,' with numerous additional charges for basic amenities like lockers and towels, strict prohibitions on outside food and drinks, and high prices for consumables inside the park. Operational issues like insufficient tubes for popular rides and concerns over sustainability policies also contribute to negative experiences.

Top positive themes

  • Fun and exciting slides
  • Cleanliness and maintenance
  • Friendly and attentive staff/lifeguards
  • Low wait times (off-peak/early)
  • Suitable for families

Top negative themes

  • High additional costs (lockers, towels, shoes)
  • No outside food/drinks & overpriced F&B
  • Lack of inflatable tubes for slides
  • Questionable sustainability practices (water policy)
  • Isolated incidents of poor staff service/theft

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer transparent ticket pricing that includes essential amenities (e.g., lockers, towels)
  • Provide more flexible and reasonably priced food and beverage options, potentially including free water refill stations or allowing outside snacks
  • Ensure ample availability of ride equipment (e.g., inflatable tubes) to minimize wait times
  • Implement clear and authentic sustainability initiatives that genuinely benefit guests and the environment
