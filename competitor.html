Customers give Vana Nava Water Jungle excellent ratings, praising the cleanliness and well-maintained slides. They had a fun time with the open rides and noted the good price. The only negative feedback was the closure of the lazy river during one visit. The customer was extremely dissatisfied with Vana Nava Water Jungle, deeming it overpriced and not worth the money.
