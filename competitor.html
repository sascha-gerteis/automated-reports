Customer voice summary

Customers overwhelmingly praise Ramayana Water Park for its fun, exciting rides, clean environment, and friendly staff. Visitors enjoy the variety of slides and the generally uncrowded atmosphere. However, significant negative feedback exists regarding high prices for food, drinks, admission for non-Thais, and souvenir photos. There are also critical complaints about inadequate locker and changing room facilities, particularly the complete lack of baby changing rooms and seating. A single reviewer had a highly positive experience, noting no wait times for adult rides, allowing them to ride multiple times and experience all adult attractions quickly. They also praised the delicious and reasonably priced food, as well as the very clean water quality. The only observation was that some attractions are strictly for families with children, preventing adult access. The sole review for Ramayana Water Park indicates extreme dissatisfaction due to the park prematurely closing and expelling customers after only three hours because of light rain. This resulted in a poor customer experience and perceived lack of value.

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket receives positive feedback for its diligent staff, strong emphasis on safety with numerous lifeguards, and a good variety of rides, leading many guests to express a desire to revisit. The park is also noted for its cleanliness. However, significant dissatisfaction arises from misleading add-ons advertised through their app, requiring guests to pay extra, and additional charges for amenities like locker and towel rentals. However, guests frequently express dissatisfaction with the food quality, limited variety (including lack of halal options), and the inability to bring outside food.
