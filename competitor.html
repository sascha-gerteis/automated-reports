Customer Voice Summary

Andamanda Phuket is praised for its "World Class Water Slides," "Friendly Staff," and overall "Clean" environment. However, significant negative feedback indicates that the park is perceived as "Overpriced." Guests are also dissatisfied with policies prohibiting outside mineral water and children's comfort items, and report that a large percentage of attractions for small children are out of order, directly impacting the family experience.

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Ensure pricing offers clear value to customers.
  • Review and clearly communicate policies regarding outside food and drink, especially for essential items like water or children's necessities.
  • Prioritize maintenance and operational status of all attractions, particularly those for younger children, to avoid customer disappointment.
  • Highlight and maintain high standards for water slide quality, staff friendliness, and park cleanliness.
