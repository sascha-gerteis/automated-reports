Customer voice summary

Ramayana Water Park is highly praised for its wide variety of thrilling slides suitable for all ages, spacious green areas, and overall clean and well-maintained environment. Guests frequently highlight the friendly, helpful, and even honest nature of the staff. However, some visitors experienced disappointment due to unannounced closures, an inconvenient payment system, high ticket prices, and occasional inconsistencies in staff responsiveness or friendliness at specific attractions.

Top positive themes

  • Variety of thrilling slides
  • Friendly and helpful staff
  • Clean and well-maintained park
  • Spacious green areas
  • Good for families and all ages

Top negative themes

  • Unannounced closures
  • Inconvenient payment system (wristbands)
  • Inconsistent staff responsiveness/friendliness
  • High ticket prices
  • Safety/maintenance incidents (broken step)

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Ensure clear communication on attraction closures or operational changes pre-visit
  • Implement flexible and convenient payment systems, allowing multiple wristbands on a single bill
  • Provide consistent training for all staff members to maintain high standards of friendliness and responsiveness
  • Offer private changing facilities within paid cabanas
  • Strategically price or package offerings to enhance perceived value for families
