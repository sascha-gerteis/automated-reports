Customer voice summary

Ramayana Water Park consistently receives fantastic feedback, with customers praising its thrilling waterslides and the absence of wait times, even allowing for multiple rides. Guests highlight the delicious and reasonably priced food options, alongside the excellent cleanliness of the water. Adult visitors appreciate being able to experience all adult rides, though some attractions are noted as exclusively for families with children. Ramayana Water Park received a single 1-star review indicating a significant issue with their operational policy. The park reportedly closed prematurely after only 3 hours due to light rain, leading to patrons being 'kicked out' and expressing dissatisfaction.

Top positive themes

  • Thrilling rides
  • Short wait times
  • Clean water quality
  • Delicious and reasonably priced food

Top negative themes

  • Early closure
  • Rain policy
  • Operating hours

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer thrilling and fun waterslide experiences
  • Implement effective queue management to minimize wait times
  • Maintain very clean water quality throughout the park
  • Provide delicious and reasonably priced food options
  • Promote clear and customer-friendly weather/rain policies

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket is highly praised for its extensive range of fun and thrilling rides, impressive size, and strong emphasis on cleanliness and maintenance. Visitors often highlight the wave pool and the friendly staff as major positives. However, significant downsides include long queues, unexpected ride closures, and a general perception of being overly money-focused due to numerous extra costs for amenities and highly-priced, restricted food and beverages, leading to a negative value sentiment.

Top positive themes

  • Fun and exciting rides
  • Large and beautiful park
  • Cleanliness and maintenance
  • Friendly staff/lifeguards
  • Wave pool

Top negative themes

  • Long wait times/crowds
  • Excessive extra costs & overpriced F&B
  • No outside food/drinks allowed
  • Inconsistent ride closures & height restrictions
  • Lack of tubes for popular slides
  • Poor food service/quality (specific incidents)
  • Security/Lost items concerns

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Clearly communicate all pricing and inclusions upfront, or offer more inclusive packages.
  • Optimize ride operations and crowd management to reduce wait times and ensure consistent ride availability.
  • Review food and beverage pricing and policies, potentially allowing outside water bottles or offering more affordable options.
  • Ensure clear and consistent communication regarding height restrictions and tube availability.
  • Prioritize security and enhance lost and found services to build guest trust.
