Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket is lauded as a clean, well-maintained water park offering exciting slides, a unique wave pool, and surfing, making it a fun destination for families and all ages. Visitors appreciate the generally short wait times, especially during off-peak periods, and the friendly, attentive staff and visible lifeguards. However, the park faces criticism for its high prices for entry and food, perceived limited attractions, and strict height/weight restrictions for rides. There are also notable negative experiences regarding food quality, portion sizes, and isolated but severe instances of unpleasant or discriminatory staff service at dining facilities, alongside concerns about security for personal belongings. Based on a single highly positive review, Andamanda Phuket is praised for being very fun and having good staff service. The reviewer intends to return with children, highlighting the waterpark's family-friendly appeal.

Top positive themes

  • Excellent for families/all ages
  • Cleanliness & maintenance
  • Exciting rides & attractions (wave pool, surfing)
  • Friendly & attentive staff
  • Short wait times (especially off-peak)
  • Fun / Enjoyment
  • Staff service

Top negative themes

  • Food quality, portion, and price issues
  • High prices / perceived poor value (entry, food, limited attractions)
  • Security concerns (lost items)
  • Strict height/weight limits for rides
  • Limited attractions for very small children

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer competitive and transparent pricing for entry, food, and amenities (e.g., towels, lockers) to enhance perceived value.
  • Ensure high-quality, reasonably priced food with consistent portion sizes and excellent service, considering options for outside water/snacks.
  • Implement robust security measures and clear communication regarding personal belongings to prevent theft and improve guest confidence.
  • Provide a diverse range of attractions suitable for all ages and sizes, clearly communicating any restrictions upfront, especially for very young children.
  • Focus on consistent, exceptional customer service across all touchpoints, particularly in food and beverage areas, to prevent isolated negative interactions.
