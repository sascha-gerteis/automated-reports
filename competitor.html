Customer voice summary

Visitors frequently praise Columbia Pictures Aquaverse for its exciting water slides, vibrant movie-themed atmosphere, live shows, and friendly staff. However, sentiment is strongly negative regarding high ticket, food, drink, and rental pricing when purchased on-site without online discounts. Additionally, guests complain about physical exhaustion from repeatedly carrying heavy tubes up multiple flights of stairs. Visitors consistently praise the park for its thrilling water slides, hourly live shows, and family-friendly atmosphere suitable for all ages. Guests noted smooth booking via Klook, great ticket value, and short wait times on weekdays. However, reviews point out that prices inside for food are high, outside food/water is strictly banned, and visitors must bring their own towels.

Top positive themes

  • Fun and varied water slides
  • Engaging entertainment, dance shows, and wave pool
  • Friendly and helpful staff
  • Thrilling water slides
  • Live shows and entertainment
  • Family-friendly activities

Top negative themes

  • Expensive food, drinks, and walk-up tickets
  • Exhausting stair climbing with heavy inflatable tubes
  • Extra charges for non-water attractions like go-karts
  • High food and drink prices
  • Strict outside food and water policy
  • Need to bring own towels

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer competitive meal bundles and more transparent F&B pricing to lessen price sensitivity.
  • Implement tube conveyor systems or assistance to reduce guest fatigue from climbing stairs.
  • Upgrade the lazy river attraction with interactive elements and stronger currents to increase its appeal.
  • Offer affordable dining options to contrast with high food prices inside the park
  • Provide clearer guidance or amenities around towel availability

Customer voice summary

Ramayana Water Park receives high praise for its wide selection of water slides, family-friendly atmosphere, clean grounds, and attentive staff service. Guests consistently highlight positive experiences during weekday visits due to shorter queues, good food options, and safe facilities. However, minor criticisms point to physical exhaustion from walking uphill, long lines during peak hours or weather delays, and expensive photo pricing. Customers express high satisfaction with Ramayana Water Park, praising its thrilling rides, clean water, and well-managed facilities. Guests highlight minimal wait times at opening and describe the park as a must-visit for families. Additionally, food inside the park is noted as delicious and reasonably priced.

Top positive themes

  • Friendly, polite, and attentive staff
  • Clean facilities, pools, and washrooms
  • Thrilling waterslides

  • Clean water and excellent facilities

Top negative themes

  • Strenuous walking distances and hill climbing to access slides

  • Promote weather-guaranteed operation policies and indoor/covered entertainment options to attract guests frustrated by open-air rain delays.
  • Promote flexible access for adults in family zones where competitor restricts adult entry.
  • Maintain fast operations and minimal wait times during opening hours.

Customer voice summary

Guests frequently praise Andamanda Phuket for its impressive wave pool, thrilling slides, and well-maintained park layout. However, visitors vent significant frustration over poor price-to-value ratio, highlighting high entry fees, costly food, paid lockers/towels, and a strict ban on outside water without free refill stations. Furthermore, serious negative reviews cite extreme staff service failures, including complaints of guest discrimination and inappropriate security behavior. Visitors praise Andamanda Phuket for its exciting rides, clean facilities, kid-friendly play areas, and helpful shuttle staff. However, multiple guests criticize the high prices for water, food, and rental items, as well as strict policies prohibiting outside food. Minor complaints also note shortages of single rider tubes and dimly lit shower facilities.

Top positive themes

  • High-quality wave pool and exciting slide variety
  • Short ride wait times and good park layout
  • Friendly and helpful pool and service staff
  • Exciting rides and kid-friendly facilities
  • Clean environment and good wave pool
  • Reliable and comfortable shuttle service

Top negative themes

  • High ticket prices and expensive add-on costs (lockers, towels, food)
  • Strict ban on outside water and lack of free hydration dispensers
  • Serious staff misconduct issues including guest discrimination and inappropriate behavior
  • Expensive food, drinks, and mandatory add-on fees
  • Shortage of single rider tubes
  • Bathroom/shower design drawbacks

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer free water refill stations and transparent ticket packages that include basic amenities like lockers to counter aggressive monetization complaints.
  • Position Aquaverse as a superior value-for-money destination with competitive pricing and quality, affordable F&B options.
  • Highlight inclusive, high-standard customer service and staff safety/professionalism to win over guests turned off by competitor service issues.
  • Capitalize on fair pricing and value inclusions for food, drinks, and rentals to contrast with competitor pricing complaints
  • Maintain high inventory levels of single-rider tubes to avoid queue bottlenecks

Customer voice summary

Vana Nava Water Jungle earns high praise for its attentive staff, abundant lifeguards, clean environment, and exciting slide variety suitable for all ages. Visitors value resort perks like free entry for Holiday Inn guests and local resident discounts. However, negative feedback highlights issues with dining options—specifically pigeons in eating areas and a lack of Halal food—as well as strict dress codes and rough wave pool floor surfaces. Recent visitors expressed high satisfaction with Vana Nava Water Jungle, praising the good pricing and convenient online booking experience via platforms like Klook. Guests also highlighted reliable overall service, adequate facilities, and short queue times during their visit.

Top positive themes

  • Attentive lifeguards and strong safety supervision
  • Exciting slides and family-friendly attractions
  • Clean facilities and low queue wait times
  • Good pricing
  • Seamless booking process
  • Short queue times

Top negative themes

  • Dining area issues and lack of Halal food options
  • Strict clothing rules and slide damage to swimwear
  • Rough eroded surface at the bottom of the wave pool

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Capitalize on food quality by offering diverse, high-quality, and Halal-certified dining choices.
  • Promote comfortable, smooth pool surfaces and clear guidelines on slide-safe swimwear to prevent customer frustration.
  • Highlight seamless guest service and fast-response safety protocols in marketing to appeal to safety-conscious families.
  • Streamline online ticket booking and redemption via partner platforms for guest convenience
  • Maintain efficient queue management to ensure short wait times for park attractions
