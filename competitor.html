Customer voice summary

Ramayana Water Park generally receives high praise for its wide variety of exciting slides, spacious and clean grounds, and fun atmosphere suitable for families. While many commend the friendly and helpful staff, there are notable concerns regarding staff unhelpfulness, particularly in managing queues or responding to incidents. Guests also find ticket prices to be a point of concern, with suggestions to book in advance. Based on a single highly positive review, customers are very satisfied with the spaciousness of the venue and the clean water quality. The park is highly recommended by this reviewer.

Customer voice summary

Customers generally have a very positive experience at Andamanda Phuket, frequently praising the variety of activities and slides, along with the seamless entry process and ease of booking via Klook. Many also highlight the clean restrooms, delicious food, and professional, friendly staff. However, a significant point of dissatisfaction arises from issues with booking cancellations and the inability to receive refunds. Additionally, the policy restricting outside beverages is a negative point for some visitors. Customers found Andamanda Phuket to be a very fun experience, with praise for good staff service. The review suggests a strong positive sentiment towards the waterpark's family-friendly atmosphere, with an explicit desire to bring children back for future visits.

  • Promote excellent park amenities such as clean facilities and delicious food options, alongside staff professionalism.
  • Review and potentially adapt policies regarding outside food and beverages, or clearly communicate in-park options and competitive pricing.
  • Ensure exceptional staff service to drive customer satisfaction.

Customer voice summary

Vana Nava Water Jungle is highly praised for its exciting rides, variety of attractions, and overall fun experience, especially during quieter periods with minimal waiting times. Many visitors, including families, found it to be a fantastic day out with dedicated children's areas. However, there are significant complaints regarding the high entrance fee and additional costs for amenities like lockers. Guests also expressed dissatisfaction with staff's English communication skills and attentiveness, citing instances of employees being on their phones.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting and fun experience
  • Variety of slides and attractions
  • Low crowds / no lines (off-peak)
  • Good for families/children
  • Delicious food

Top negative themes

  • High entrance fees / extra costs
  • Poor staff communication (English)
  • Inattentive staff (on phones)
  • Prohibition of outside food/drinks

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer transparent and competitive pricing, avoiding hidden extra costs for basic amenities.
  • Ensure highly trained staff with excellent English communication skills and attentive customer service.
  • Highlight family-friendly value propositions, potentially through bundled deals or clear all-inclusive options.
