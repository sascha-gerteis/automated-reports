Customer voice summary

Guests enjoy Columbia Pictures Aquaverse for its exciting slides, fun movie themes, live shows, and friendly staff, making it a hit with children and families. However, visitors frequently complain about high prices for food, drinks, rentals, and walk-up tickets, as well as the tiring physical effort required to climb up multiple floors for slide access. Purchasing discounted tickets online in advance is strongly recommended by visitors. Visitors consistently rate Columbia Pictures Aquaverse highly for its thrilling water slides, entertaining live shows, and family-friendly atmosphere suitable for all ages. Online booking is highlighted as easy and affordable, with low queue times during weekdays. The main customer drawback is the steep cost of food inside alongside strict rules prohibiting outside food and drinks.

Customer voice summary

Customers praise Ramayana Water Park for its high safety standards, extremely helpful staff, clean facilities, and wide selection of thrilling slides for all ages. However, visitors express frustration over long queue times, extensive walking distances between zones, and unexpected additional costs for lockers, towels, and photos. Occasional technical ride breakdowns and weather delays also lead to negative feedback during peak times. Visitors consistently praise Ramayana Water Park as a well-managed, clean, and exciting destination for families. Guests appreciate the lack of long lines when visiting early, allowing multiple rides on thrilling waterslides. Additionally, the on-site dining is highlighted for being both delicious and reasonably priced.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting and varied water slides
  • Clean facilities and pools
  • Friendly and helpful staff
  • Short wait times and minimal queues
  • Thrilling slides and excellent facilities
  • Clean water quality and good park management

Top negative themes

  • Long queue lines for popular rides
  • Extra charges for add-ons like lockers and towels
  • Technical issues and ride closures

  • Promote all-inclusive ticket options that cover lockers, towels, and digital photos to counter guest frustration with extra fees.

  • Capitalize on seamless navigation and shaded walkways to attract guests looking to avoid long walks in the heat.
  • Offer competitive food pricing and quality to match positive sentiment around Ramayana's dining.
  • Promote water cleanliness, safety, and excellent facility maintenance standards.

Customer voice summary

Guests strongly praise Andamanda Phuket for its vast, clean grounds, exciting slides, wave pool, and attentive staff. However, sentiment is weighed down by excessive add-on costs for lockers, towels, and overpriced food, alongside strict policies against outside drinks. Operational bottlenecks like tube shortages, long queues, and strict height restrictions for young children also cause frustration. Andamanda Phuket receives strong positive feedback for its exciting ride variety, clean park environment, and exceptional shuttle transfer service. Guests appreciate the short line waits and family-friendly offerings for both adults and young children. However, minor criticisms point to extra costs for towels, shortages of single rider tubes, and shower facilities lacking hooks and sufficient lighting.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting slides and ride variety
  • Attentive lifeguards and friendly staff
  • Clean and well-maintained facilities
  • Excellent shuttle service and helpful staff
  • Great facilities for both kids and adults

Top negative themes

  • High extra charges for lockers, towels, and food
  • Long queue times and shortages of inflatable tubes
  • Strict height restrictions for young children
  • Shortage of single rider tubes
  • Extra charges for amenities like towels
  • Shower and toilet facility minor flaws

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Promote all-inclusive ticket packages that cover lockers and towels to appeal to value-conscious visitors frustrated by add-on fees.
  • Optimize tube availability and ride queue management to prevent double-waiting for inflatable tubes.
  • Provide clear upfront height guidelines and expand inclusive, fun attractions for toddlers and younger children.
  • Ensure ample availability of single-rider tubes across all slide towers
  • Provide comfortable shower amenities with hooks, ample lighting, and complimentary towels

Customer voice summary

Guests consistently praise Vana Nava Water Jungle for its exciting slides, clean facilities, short wait times, and exceptionally helpful staff and lifeguards. However, negative feedback highlights issues with food quality, lack of Halal options, pigeons in the dining space, and minor maintenance concerns like a sharp wave pool floor. Customers express high satisfaction with Vana Nava Water Jungle, particularly praising the seamless booking experience via Klook, good pricing, and short queue times. Reviewers also compliment the service quality and note that it is a great attraction for both kids and adults.

Top positive themes

  • Attentive staff and high safety/first-aid standards
  • Exciting slides with minimal queue times
  • Clean facilities and family-friendly atmosphere
  • Easy online booking process
  • Good pricing and value
  • Short queue times

Top negative themes

  • Pests in dining area (pigeons)
  • Lack of Halal food options and mediocre food quality
  • Pool maintenance concerns (rough/sharp surface in wave pool)

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Promote diverse and inclusive dining options, including certified Halal choices, to cater to international travelers.
  • Capitalize on superior pest control and clean, comfortable dining environments free from nuisance birds.
  • Highlight a broader range of high-thrill adult attractions to appeal to guests looking for more than a few major slides.
  • Ensure seamless third-party online booking integrations (e.g. Klook) for frictionless customer entry
  • Maintain efficient queue management to keep wait times low during peak periods
