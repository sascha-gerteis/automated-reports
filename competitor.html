Customer voice summary

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is praised for its thrilling water slides, energetic wave pool, movie-themed branding, and attentive staff. However, sentiment is significantly dampened by high food and beverage prices, expensive gate tickets, and add-on charges for extra activities. Visitors also consistently complain about the physically exhausting climb up multi-story stairs to reach the slides while carrying heavy tubes. Visitors report high satisfaction with Columbia Pictures Aquaverse, highlighting thrilling water slides, diverse attraction zones, and high-quality live shows suitable for families. Reviewers appreciate good ticket deals via platforms like Klook and short lines on weekdays. However, guests caution that in-park food and water prices are steep, outside refreshments are prohibited, and visitors should bring their own towels.

Top positive themes

  • Fun and thrilling water slides
  • Movie-themed atmosphere and live entertainment
  • Friendly and helpful staff service
  • Thrilling water slides and rides
  • Live shows and entertainment
  • Great family atmosphere for all ages
  • Affordable ticket prices and easy booking

Top negative themes

  • Expensive food and beverage prices
  • Tiring stair climbs and heavy tubes
  • High walk-up ticket costs and extra fees for activities
  • High inside food and drink costs
  • Strict outside food and water policy
  • Must bring your own towels

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Promote affordable and value-driven food and beverage offerings to attract price-sensitive visitors
  • Highlight easier park navigation, comfortable stair access, or tube lifting systems to contrast against Aquaverse's tiring climbs
  • Offer transparent, all-inclusive ticketing options that eliminate hidden add-on costs for key attractions
  • Address complaints about expensive in-park dining by offering meal bundles or F&B discounts.
  • Provide towel rental options or clearer pre-visit communications reminding guests to bring towels.

Customer voice summary

Guests consistently praise Ramayana Water Park for its wide array of thrilling slides, clean grounds, and exceptionally polite and attentive staff. Family visitors and international tourists highlight weekday convenience, low queue times, and free photo emails as key highlights. Minor criticisms focus on long walking distances between zones, heavy queue congestion following ride breakdowns or rain delays, and paid photographer photos. Visitors express high satisfaction with Ramayana Water Park, praising its thrilling waterslides, clean water, and well-managed facilities. Guests appreciate minimal wait times when arriving at park opening, allowing them to experience rides multiple times. Additionally, the food is highlighted as delicious and reasonably priced, making the park a strongly recommended choice for families.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting variety of slides
  • Friendly and attentive staff
  • Clean facilities and pools
  • Short wait times for rides
  • Thrilling waterslides and excellent facilities
  • Delicious and reasonably priced food

Top negative themes

  • Long queues during peak times
  • Long walking distances and hill climbs
  • Ride delays from weather and technical issues

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Promote seamless ride operations and transparent weather policies to avoid guest disappointment during ride closures.
  • Highlight park layout convenience, shaded walkways, and comfortable rest areas to counter physical fatigue reported at rival parks.
  • Provide competitive guest perks such as multi-language signage (including Russian) and inclusive digital media options.
  • Highlight short line wait times and efficient ride operations during morning hours.
  • Promote competitively priced, high-quality food and beverage offerings.

Customer voice summary

Visitors praise Andamanda Phuket for its high-quality rides, impressive wave pool, and fun adrenaline options. However, customer sentiment is heavily impacted by perceived overcharging on tickets, lockers, towels, and food, as well as strict policies banning outside drinking water. Serious negative feedback also highlights instances of staff harassment, discrimination, and inadequate shade leading to severe sunburns. Guests praise Andamanda Phuket for its diverse slide selection, family-friendly facilities, short lines, and exceptional shuttle transport. Minor complaints include a shortage of single-rider tubes, cool water temperatures, and dark restrooms lacking hooks for towels. Additionally, guests noted that many amenities require additional payment.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting slides and large wave pool
  • Friendly frontline staff and lifeguards
  • Well-organized layout and short queues early in the day
  • Exciting slides and ride variety
  • Excellent shuttle transfer service
  • Short wait times and low crowds

Top negative themes

  • High admission, locker, and towel rental fees
  • Strict outside water ban and lack of free drinking water
  • Incidents of staff misconduct and discrimination
  • Shortage of single rider tubes
  • Extra charges for amenities like towels
  • Dark restrooms lacking shower hooks

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Capitalize on fair pricing by offering bundled ticket options with free locker access and free water refill stations.
  • Emphasize guest comfort by providing extensive shade, sun safety awareness, and inclusive non-swimmer/pregnant spectator ticket options.
  • Promote a zero-tolerance customer service standard with well-trained, respectful staff to contrast competitor service missteps.
  • Ensure an abundant supply of single-rider tubes to eliminate guest wait friction
  • Provide well-lit, fully equipped restrooms and shower facilities with plenty of hooks and shelf space

Customer voice summary

Vana Nava Water Jungle receives strong praise for its clean facilities, thrilling slides, short queues, and exceptionally attentive safety staff and medical personnel. Families and groups highly rate the overall atmosphere and value packages. However, recurring complaints focus on dining facilities, specifically poor food quality, a total lack of Halal choices, slow swim-up bar service, and pigeons in the eating areas. Recent guest reviews for Vana Nava Water Jungle highlight a highly positive experience focused on smooth booking via Klook and overall great service. Visitors praise the affordable prices, short queue times, and good facilities. The park is praised as a great location suitable for both children and adults.

Top positive themes

  • Attentive lifeguards and exceptional medical/first-aid care
  • Thrilling slides and unique VR slide experiences
  • Clean facilities and short queue times
  • Easy online booking
  • Good pricing and value
  • Short queue times

Top negative themes

  • Pigeons in the outdoor dining area
  • Lack of Halal food options and low food quality
  • Sharp/eroded surface at the wave pool bottom

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer and clearly advertise certified Halal food options to capture international and Muslim tourists missing out at competitors.
  • Maintain strict pest control and sanitary measures in dining areas to avoid bird issues experienced by competitors.
  • Highlight superior food quality, diverse dining choices, and fast service in marketing campaigns.
  • Streamline third-party booking integrations to ensure fast, reliable ticketing for guests
  • Promote competitive pricing and bundled ticket options to enhance value perception
