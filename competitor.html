Customer voice summary

Customers generally find Columbia Pictures Aquaverse to be a fun and exciting water park with a variety of themed slides and activities, particularly enjoyable for families and kids, and appreciate the friendly staff. However, significant concerns arise from the high cost of admission and overwhelmingly overpriced food and drinks. Guests also frequently express frustration over the requirement to carry heavy tubes up multiple flights of stairs and report some rides feeling run down or having safety issues. Based on a single review, Columbia Pictures Aquaverse received an average rating of 3 out of 5. The primary criticisms centered around the perceived value, with the price not being justified, and the park's suitability, which was noted to be more appropriate for older adults rather than potentially younger audiences or families.

Top negative themes

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Address the inconvenience of guests carrying heavy tubes up to slides by providing staff assistance or mechanical lifts.
  • Re-evaluate pricing strategy for entrance fees, food, and beverages to offer better value and address customer complaints about high costs.
  • Invest in ride maintenance and upgrades to improve perceived condition and ensure smooth, uninterrupted operation.
  • Improve guest amenities such as providing comfortable indoor seating (especially for families with young children) and Wi-Fi access.
  • Review and refine safety protocols for rides to prevent incidents like guests getting stuck and to ensure clear communication of weight restrictions.
