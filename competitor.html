Customer voice summary

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is generally enjoyed by families and thrill-seekers for its variety of slides, engaging shows, and attentive staff. However, a pervasive negative theme is the high cost of everything from tickets and food to rentals, leading to poor perceived value. Visitors also frequently complain about the physical effort required to carry heavy tubes up numerous stairs for adult rides and some perceive the park as smaller compared to competitors. Columbia Pictures Aquaverse consistently receives fantastic reviews, with customers highly praising the thrilling water slides, the wide variety of attractions including stunning shows and interactive games, and its overall appeal for families. Guests appreciate the easy booking process and good ticket prices. However, a recurring point of concern is the strict policy against bringing in outside food and water, coupled with the perceived high cost of food and beverages inside the park.

  • High internal food/drink costs

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Review pricing strategy for tickets, F&B, and rentals to enhance perceived value.
  • Implement solutions for carrying tubes on adult slides (e.g., lifts, staff assistance) to improve guest comfort.
  • Offer more reasonably priced food and drink options, or revisit the outside water policy for families.
  • Address specific water quality concerns and consider renovations for public areas like changing rooms.
  • Emphasize unique offerings or consider adding attractions to counter comparisons with competitors.
