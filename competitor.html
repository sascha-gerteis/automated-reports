Customer voice summary

Ramayana Water Park is highly praised for its thrilling and diverse slides, spacious layout, and friendly, attentive staff, often with minimal queues. However, a recurring point of dissatisfaction is the high pricing for food, drinks, non-Thai admission, and souvenir photos. A significant concern raised by guests is the inadequate changing room facilities, particularly the complete absence of baby changing rooms and insufficient seating or storage.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting slides and rides
  • Friendly and attentive staff
  • Not crowded / short queues
  • Cleanliness
  • Spacious park area

Top negative themes

  • Expensive food and drinks
  • Inadequate changing room facilities
  • Lack of baby changing facilities
  • Expensive admission for non-Thais
  • Expensive souvenir photos

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer superior and family-friendly changing room facilities, including dedicated baby changing rooms.
  • Ensure competitive and perceived value in pricing for food, beverages, and other amenities like photos.
  • Maintain consistently high standards of cleanliness across all park areas, including pathways.
  • Highlight staff attentiveness and friendliness as a key differentiator in customer experience.
  • Promote a wide variety of thrilling slides and attractions with efficient queue management.

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket generally receives high praise for its exciting rides, overall cleanliness, and well-maintained facilities. Many visitors highlight the friendly staff and enjoyable experience, especially during off-peak hours with shorter wait times. However, common frustrations stem from high additional costs for amenities like lockers and towels, overpriced food and drinks, and strict no-outside-food policies, which lead to a perception of money-focused management. Other issues include occasional long waits for popular rides or tubes, and inconsistent experiences with staff regarding height restrictions or general helpfulness. Andamanda Phuket is praised for being a family-friendly waterpark with a strong emphasis on safety, numerous lifeguards, and a good variety of rides. Guests appreciate the clean environment and nice staff. However, significant negative feedback revolves around hidden costs for essential amenities like lockers and towels, as well as problems with third-party app bookings falsely advertising inclusions.

Top positive themes

  • Cleanliness & maintenance
  • Friendly staff & good service
  • safety and lifeguards
  • family-friendly experience
  • cleanliness and staff
  • variety of rides

Top negative themes

  • High costs & F&B restrictions
  • Waiting times (rides & tubes)
  • Inconsistent service & policies
  • hidden costs for amenities
  • issues with app bookings
  • water temperature

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer transparent and inclusive pricing for basic amenities and F&B.
  • Implement efficient queue management and ensure ample availability of ride tubes.
  • Ensure consistent and compassionate staff training, particularly regarding guest concerns and children's policies.
  • Offer transparent pricing with amenities (e.g., towels, lockers) included or clearly communicated upfront
  • Ensure a reliable and accurate booking experience, especially for add-ons and package deals

Customer voice summary

Vana Nava Water Jungle is highly praised for its thrilling rides, fun atmosphere, and exceptional staff service and attentiveness, including excellent customer and medical care. Guests consistently highlight the park's cleanliness, short queue times, and family-friendly environment. However, recurring concerns exist regarding the quality and limited options of food, particularly the absence of halal choices, and some perceive the pricing as a bit expensive. Customers find Vana Nava Water Jungle to be a fantastic destination, highlighting the ease of booking via Klook and excellent facilities. It is praised as a great place for both children and adults. The customer found Vana Nava Water Jungle to be extremely overpriced and a disappointing experience. Key frustrations included a lack of clear information regarding attraction access for an 11-year-old child before payment, a problematic wristband payment system, and expensive, cold food with long wait times. The park also felt poorly organized, leading to an inconvenient overall visit.
