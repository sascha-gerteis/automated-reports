Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket is lauded for its fantastic slides, variety of attractions, and overall cleanliness, making it a great destination for families. However, a significant point of contention for visitors is the perceived high pricing for food, drinks, and additional amenities like lockers, coupled with a strict no outside food or drink policy. Other issues include insufficient tubes leading to long waits and some negative experiences with staff professionalism. Andamanda Phuket receives overwhelmingly positive feedback, with customers highly appreciating the variety and fun of its rides, short wait times, and excellent shuttle service and staff. The park is praised for being clean and offering plenty of activities for families. However, guests note a shortage of single rider tubes, minor issues with shower and toilet facilities (darkness, lack of hooks), and mention that the water could be warmer, alongside having to pay for extras like towels.

Top positive themes

  • Cleanliness and beautiful park grounds

  • Friendly and welcoming staff (general)

Top negative themes

  • Lack of tubes for popular rides and long waits
  • Inconsistent ride availability and occasional long lines
  • Instances of unprofessional or unempathetic staff
  • Shortage of single rider tubes
  • Shower/toilet facilities (dark, lacking hooks)
  • Water temperature too cool

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer transparent and competitive pricing for food, drinks, and amenities, possibly including some basics in the ticket price.
  • Implement clear and customer-friendly policies regarding outside food and drinks, or provide more affordable F&B options.
  • Ensure ample availability of tubes and efficient line management for popular rides.
  • Provide comprehensive staff training on professional conduct, empathy, and consistent customer service across all roles.
  • Clearly communicate ride restrictions and consider alternative offerings or pricing for guests unable to participate in most attractions.
