Customer voice summary

Customers overwhelmingly praise Ramayana Water Park for its fun, exciting rides, clean environment, and friendly staff. Visitors enjoy the variety of slides and the generally uncrowded atmosphere. However, significant negative feedback exists regarding high prices for food, drinks, admission for non-Thais, and souvenir photos. There are also critical complaints about inadequate locker and changing room facilities, particularly the complete lack of baby changing rooms and seating. A single reviewer had a highly positive experience, noting no wait times for adult rides, allowing them to ride multiple times and experience all adult attractions quickly. They also praised the delicious and reasonably priced food, as well as the very clean water quality. The only observation was that some attractions are strictly for families with children, preventing adult access.

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket receives positive feedback for its diligent staff, strong emphasis on safety with numerous lifeguards, and a good variety of rides, leading many guests to express a desire to revisit. The park is also noted for its cleanliness. However, significant dissatisfaction arises from misleading add-ons advertised through their app, requiring guests to pay extra, and additional charges for amenities like locker and towel rentals. One review also mentioned the water being too cold.

Customer voice summary

Vana Nava Water Jungle is highly praised for its clean environment, attentive staff, and exciting rides, offering a fun experience with often short wait times. However, guests frequently express dissatisfaction with the food quality, limited variety (including lack of halal options), and the inability to bring outside food. There are also mentions of the park being a bit expensive and a specific concern about wave pool erosion. Based on a single 5-star review, Vana Nava Water Jungle is highly praised for its facilities and being a great place for both kids and adults. The ease of booking, specifically through Klook, was also highlighted as a positive aspect. The customer found Vana Nava Water Jungle to be extremely overpriced and a disappointing experience. Key complaints included a lack of transparency regarding attraction access for an 11-year-old child after payment, issues with the wristband payment system, and poor food quality characterized by long waits, high cost, and cold dishes. Overall, the park was perceived as poorly organized with inconvenient facilities and below-par service.
