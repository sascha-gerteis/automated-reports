Customer voice summary

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse offers fun, thrilling slides and a spacious, movie-themed environment great for families and kids, with some guests praising helpful staff and interactive shows. However, the experience is frequently marred by the significant inconvenience of guests having to carry heavy tubes up multiple flights of stairs. High prices for admission, food, and drinks, along with inconsistent staff attitudes and safety concerns on rides, are also significant detractors.

Top positive themes

  • Fun and exciting slides
  • Family-friendly attractions/shows
  • Spacious and well-themed zones
  • Helpful and welcoming staff (specific instances)
  • Good deals on tickets/vouchers (when available)

Top negative themes

  • Inconvenience of carrying heavy tubes
  • High prices for admission, food, and drinks
  • Inconsistent staff attitude/service
  • Park conditions/maintenance (run down)
  • Strict outside food/drink and re-entry policies
  • Safety concerns on rides

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Implement a system for transporting tubes to slide entry points to improve guest comfort and safety.
  • Review and adjust pricing strategies for admission, food, and beverages, or offer more accessible value bundles.
  • Provide comprehensive staff training on customer service, empathy, and assistance, particularly concerning physical help and policy enforcement.
  • Address reported safety incidents and overall ride maintenance to ensure consistent guest safety.
  • Consider adding amenities like Wi-Fi and cooled indoor seating areas for guest comfort.

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket receives widespread praise for its exceptional cleanliness, thrilling water rides, and the friendly, attentive service from its staff, often leading to enjoyable family experiences with short wait times during off-peak visits. However, significant customer dissatisfaction stems from the perceived high cost for entry and food, with many feeling the park offers limited attractions for the price. Specific negative feedback also highlights issues with food quality, poor staff interactions at dining outlets, and security concerns regarding lost personal items. Customers found Andamanda Phuket to be very fun, specifically praising the good service provided by the staff. The reviewer intends to bring children back, suggesting a positive experience for families.

Top positive themes

  • Staff friendliness & service
  • Cleanliness & maintenance
  • Ride variety & excitement
  • Family-friendly experience
  • Low crowds & short wait times
  • Fun
  • Good Service

Top negative themes

  • Value for money / high prices
  • Food quality & restaurant service issues
  • Limited attractions & park size perception
  • Safety & security concerns (lost items)
  • Strict restrictions (height, weight, outside food)

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Clearly communicate value proposition and potentially offer competitive pricing or package deals.
  • Elevate food and beverage quality, portion sizes, and service consistency at all dining outlets.
  • Enhance on-site security measures and streamline the lost-and-found process to build guest trust.
  • Review and clearly communicate attraction suitability and restrictions for various age groups and sizes.
  • Consider flexible policies on outside food/water or provide more affordable, high-quality alternatives within the park.
