Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket is highly praised for its extensive selection of new and varied rides and its strong emphasis on safety, with guests frequently noting the abundance of vigilant lifeguards. Many visitors reported a seamless entry and a fantastic overall experience, highlighting the park's family-friendly nature. However, a significant concern was raised regarding misleading add-ons when purchasing through their app, leading to customers having to pay extra for supposedly included items, despite third-party apps like Klook offering good value. Based on a single recent review, customers found Andamanda Phuket to be very fun, specifically praising the good service provided by the staff. There's an expressed intent to return with children, highlighting a positive family-friendly experience.
