Customer voice summary

Guests consistently highlight Columbia Pictures Aquaverse for its exciting themed slides, wave pool entertainment, and friendly staff. However, customer satisfaction is heavily impacted by overpriced food and beverage options, high walk-up ticket costs, and additional fees for non-water attractions. Furthermore, visitors frequently note the exhaustion of carrying heavy tubes up multiple stories to reach the main rides. Visitors consistently highlight Columbia Pictures Aquaverse for its thrilling water slides, themed zones like Zombieland and Jaguar Slides, and hourly entertainment shows suitable for both adults and teenagers. Guests appreciate affordable ticket prices and smooth booking via Klook, as well as minimal wait times on weekdays. However, reviews note that outside food and drinks are strictly forbidden and inside food costs are steep.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting slides and fun adult/kid zones
  • Engaging movie themes, wave pool, and live shows
  • Friendly and helpful staff service
  • Thrilling water slides and rides
  • Entertaining live shows
  • Affordable ticket prices and easy booking

Top negative themes

  • Expensive food, drinks, and mandatory VAT additions
  • Exhausting stair climbs and heavy inflatable tubes
  • High gate ticket prices and separate fees for extra activities
  • High cost of food and drinks inside
  • Strict outside food and water policy
  • Need to bring your own towels

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Address physical strain by implementing tube lift systems or stair relief solutions for high-story slide towers.
  • Adjust food and beverage pricing or introduce bundled meal packages to improve guest value perception.
  • Promote clearer messaging around online discount tickets and integrated access packages for add-on activities like go-karts.
  • Offer more reasonably priced in-park food and beverage options
  • Provide clear guidance or rental services for towels

Customer voice summary

Guests consistently enjoy Andamanda Phuket's impressive wave pool, fun slides, and friendly staff. However, the overall sentiment is severely impacted by high overall costs, expensive food, and mandatory charges for lockers and towels. Visitors also strongly criticize the strict ban on outside water coupled with overpriced bottled water on site. Visitors generally love Andamanda Phuket for its exciting rides, clean facilities, kid-friendly environment, and high-quality shuttle service. However, there is strong customer dissatisfaction regarding expensive add-ons, high food and drink prices, and strict policies banning outside food. Some guests also noted issues with overcrowding and a shortage of single-rider tubes.

Top positive themes

  • Large wave pool and thrilling slide variety
  • Friendly, welcoming, and attentive staff
  • Clean and well-maintained facilities
  • Exciting rides and kid-friendly attractions
  • Praiseworthy and punctual shuttle/transfer service
  • Clean park facilities and short wait times

Top negative themes

  • High ticket, locker, and towel rental prices
  • Strict outside water ban with lack of free drinking water
  • Overpriced and low-quality food options
  • High prices for seating, water, and paid extras
  • Strict policy against outside food and drinks
  • Shortage of single rider tubes and occasional overcrowding

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer transparent, high-value ticket bundles that include lockers, towels, and food vouchers to counter competitor price fatigue.
  • Promote guest-friendly water and hydration policies, such as complimentary water refill stations throughout the park.
  • Highlight family-friendly policies with clear height accessibility and discounted/special tickets for non-riding companions or expectant mothers.
  • Promote transparent pricing and free seating or included amenities to directly address guest frustration with add-on fees.
  • Offer affordable food and beverage options or flexible dining policies to contrast with strict and expensive food rules.

Customer voice summary

Vana Nava Water Jungle receives praise for its thrilling slides, clean facilities, short wait times, and outstanding customer service, particularly regarding lifeguard vigilance and medical care. Visitors frequently highlight the park's suitability for families and good value through ticket and hotel packages. However, recurring complaints mention subpar food quality, lack of halal options, birds in the dining area, and damaged swimwear due to slide rules. Recent reviews highlight strong satisfaction driven primarily by an easy online booking process and good pricing. Guests also praised reliable service, short queue times, and adequate facilities.

Top positive themes

  • Attentive lifeguards and high safety standards
  • Exciting slides and family-friendly attractions
  • Clean facilities and short wait times
  • Easy booking process
  • Good pricing
  • Short queue times

Top negative themes

  • Pests and quality issues in dining areas
  • Lack of halal food options
  • Strict swimwear/attire restrictions and slide damage

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer diverse, high-quality dining, including clearly labeled Halal options and pest-free eating environments.
  • Ensure clear, proactive swimwear guidelines to prevent guest clothing damage on slides.
  • Capitalize on wave pool surface maintenance, addressing worn pool floors that competitors neglect.
  • Ensure smooth and convenient online ticket booking processes
  • Highlight competitive pricing and ticket value in promotions
