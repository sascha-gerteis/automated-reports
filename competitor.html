Customer voice summary

Customers generally praise Columbia Pictures Aquaverse for its fun environment and diverse range of attractions, including water slides, live shows, and a lazy river, making it suitable for families. Booking is frequently noted as easy and convenient, often with good ticket prices. However, recurring criticisms revolve around strict policies prohibiting outside food and water, coupled with steep costs for items purchased within the park, leading to mixed feelings about overall value.

Top positive themes

  • Easy booking
  • Variety of attractions
  • Family-friendly

Top negative themes

  • Steep internal costs
  • Strict outside food/water policy
  • Value for money

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Re-evaluate internal pricing and policies regarding outside food/water to improve value perception and customer experience.
  • Address the perception that the park might be 'more suitable for older adults' if aiming for a broader demographic.
  • Continue to highlight the ease of booking and the wide variety of attractions in marketing efforts.

Customer voice summary

Customers overwhelmingly praise Ramayana Water Park for its fun and thrilling experience, wide variety of slides and attractions suitable for all ages, and its clean, beautiful environment supported by friendly and attentive staff. However, recurring feedback highlights dissatisfaction with the weak wave pool and perceived high prices for food, admission, and souvenir photos, alongside a desire for longer operating hours. Guests at Ramayana Water Park enjoyed immediate access to adult rides, allowing them to ride multiple times within a short visit. The food was praised for being delicious and reasonably priced, and the water quality was noted as very clean. The only point of note was that some attractions are strictly for families with children, restricting adult access.

Top positive themes

  • Variety of Rides & Thrills
  • Friendly & Attentive Staff
  • Clean & Beautiful Environment
  • No wait times for rides
  • Ability to ride multiple times
  • Delicious and reasonably priced food
  • Clean water quality

Top negative themes

  • High Prices (Food, Admission, Photos)
  • Weak Wave Pool Experience
  • Short Operating Hours
  • Adults restricted from some family attractions

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer competitive pricing and value packages for food, beverages, admission, and souvenirs.
  • Develop a superior and more dynamic wave pool experience with noticeable waves.
  • Provide extended operating hours to allow guests more time to enjoy the park fully.
  • Implement clear and visible guest safety information, such as weight limits for all slides, to enhance safety perception.
  • Highlight inclusive ride access for all adult guests across most attractions

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket is praised for its large, beautiful grounds, exciting and varied slides suitable for all ages, and high standards of cleanliness, safety, and maintenance. However, significant concerns revolve around the park's perceived money-focused approach, with numerous extra costs for amenities and high internal food/drink prices coupled with a strict no-outside policy. Other issues include inconsistent children's height restrictions, a lack of tubes causing delays, and some instances of unfriendly or discriminatory staff behavior.

Top positive themes

  • Large park & beautiful grounds
  • Exciting slides & variety for all ages
  • Cleanliness & good maintenance
  • Ample lifeguards & staff service
  • Short wait times (off-peak)

Top negative themes

  • High additional costs (lockers, towels, shoes)
  • Strict no outside food/drinks & high F&B prices
  • Inconsistent height restrictions for children
  • Lack of tubes for popular slides
  • Unfriendly or discriminatory staff instances

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer transparent pricing with essential amenities included or bundled.
  • Allow outside water bottles and provide reasonably priced F&B options.
  • Ensure clear, consistent communication on height restrictions and staff empathy.
  • Optimize tube availability for popular slides to minimize wait times.
  • Implement robust security measures for guest belongings.

Customer voice summary

Vana Nava Water Jungle is highly praised for its thrilling rides, exceptional staff attentiveness, and overall cleanliness, with many guests enjoying short wait times. It is considered a fun and suitable destination for families and adults alike. However, consistent feedback highlights dissatisfaction with the food quality, limited variety, and specifically the absence of halal options, which is a notable pain point for some visitors. The single reviewer for Vana Nava Water Jungle reported an extremely negative experience, primarily citing the park as overpriced and not worth the money. Key frustrations included a lack of clear information about age restrictions for attractions before payment, a problematic wristband payment system, and disappointing food quality which was expensive, cold, and slow. The park's overall organization and convenience, including the location of amenities, were also criticized.

Top positive themes

  • Thrilling rides and slides
  • Attentive and friendly staff
  • Cleanliness and maintenance
  • Short wait times/minimal queues
  • Family-friendly atmosphere

Top negative themes

  • Food quality and variety
  • Lack of halal food options
  • Wave pool erosion
  • Perceived high price
  • Limited ride variety (for some)
  • Pricing & Value
  • Unclear Information (Age Restrictions)
  • Payment System Issues

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer diverse, high-quality, and culturally inclusive food options, including halal.
  • Ensure consistent maintenance of all facilities, proactively addressing wear and tear on attractions like wave pools.
  • Communicate clear value propositions or package deals to address perceptions of high cost.
  • Highlight a broad range of attractions catering to different thrill levels and age groups beyond 'thrilling and scary' rides.
  • Provide clear and transparent information on all attraction rules and age restrictions before ticket purchase.
