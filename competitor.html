Customer voice summary

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse offers fun, thrilling slides and a spacious, movie-themed environment great for families and kids, with some guests praising helpful staff and interactive shows. However, the experience is frequently marred by the significant inconvenience of guests having to carry heavy tubes up multiple flights of stairs. High prices for admission, food, and drinks, along with inconsistent staff attitudes and safety concerns on rides, are also significant detractors.

Top positive themes

Top negative themes

  • Inconvenience of carrying heavy tubes
  • High prices for admission, food, and drinks
  • Inconsistent staff attitude/service
  • Park conditions/maintenance (run down)
  • Strict outside food/drink and re-entry policies
  • Safety concerns on rides

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Implement a system for transporting tubes to slide entry points to improve guest comfort and safety.

  • Provide comprehensive staff training on customer service, empathy, and assistance, particularly concerning physical help and policy enforcement.
  • Address reported safety incidents and overall ride maintenance to ensure consistent guest safety.
  • Consider adding amenities like Wi-Fi and cooled indoor seating areas for guest comfort.
