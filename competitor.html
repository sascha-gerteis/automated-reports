Customer voice summary

Customers generally find Columbia Pictures Aquaverse a fun and impressive water park with thrilling rides, a wide variety of activities, and excellent movie-themed decor offering many photo opportunities. The frontline staff are frequently praised for being welcoming, helpful, and ensuring safety. However, common criticisms revolve around the high prices of food and drinks, various management issues including ride safety concerns, a strict re-entry policy, and unhelpful phone service.

Top positive themes

Top negative themes

  • High food & drink prices

Guests appreciate the seamless entry process, clean restrooms, delicious food, and the friendly, professional staff.
