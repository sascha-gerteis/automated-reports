Customer voice summary

Ramayana Water Park is highly praised for its wide variety of exciting slides for all ages, spacious and well-maintained green grounds, and overall fun atmosphere. Many visitors highlighted the staff's general friendliness and helpfulness, with one notable instance of honesty. However, some guests experienced disappointment due to unannounced facility closures, issues with staff responsiveness (e.g., after an injury, or regarding queue management), and perceived high ticket prices for families.
