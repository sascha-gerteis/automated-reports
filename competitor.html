Customer voice summary

Visitors generally praise Columbia Pictures Aquaverse for its exciting themed slides, clean atmosphere, engaging wave pool, and friendly staff suitable for all age groups. However, there is substantial dissatisfaction around high prices for food, drinks, lockers, and towels. Additionally, guests frequently complain about physical fatigue from carrying heavy tubes up many flights of stairs and encountering hot walking surfaces. Visitors report high satisfaction with Columbia Pictures Aquaverse, highlighting thrilling water slides, quality hourly shows, and minimal wait times. The park is considered great for both adults and older children/teenagers. However, guests express dissatisfaction with expensive in-park food costs and strict outside food/beverage restrictions.

Top positive themes

  • Great entertainment and wave pool
  • Clean facilities and friendly staff

  • Entertaining hourly shows

Top negative themes

  • Promote low wait times and hourly show schedules to attract families and teenagers

Customer voice summary

Visitors frequently compliment Andamanda Phuket for its thrilling slides, massive wave pool, clean facilities, and highly attentive lifeguard team. However, customer satisfaction is impacted by high ticket prices, expensive food of varying quality, and additional fees for lockers and towels. Guests are particularly frustrated by the strict policy prohibiting outside water bottles and height restrictions that restrict toddlers from most attractions. Customers overall enjoy Andamanda Phuket for its exciting rides, clean facilities, kid-friendly attractions, and helpful shuttle transfer service. However, there is significant frustration regarding excessive add-on costs, including high prices for seating, water, and accessories, along with a strict no-outside-food policy. Additionally, overcrowding and a shortage of single-rider tubes affected the experience for some guests.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting slides and impressive wave pool
  • Attentive staff and high safety focus
  • Clean, modern, and well-maintained park layout
  • Exciting slides and wave pool
  • Kid-friendly facilities and activities
  • Punctual and helpful shuttle service

Top negative themes

  • High admission, food, and beverage prices
  • Strict ban on outside water and reusable bottles
  • Height restrictions limiting attractions for young kids
  • High and exploitative add-on pricing
  • Strict outside food/drink ban with expensive internal options
  • Overcrowding and shortage of single rider tubes

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Promote family-friendly pricing with clear height brackets and strong toddler-inclusive value.
  • Allow reusable water bottles or provide accessible free water refill stations to counter competitor restriction complaints.
  • Emphasize high-quality, competitively priced food options and bundle extras like lockers to highlight superior overall value.
  • Offer transparent and reasonably priced food, drinks, and essential amenities to contrast with competitor pricing complaints
  • Ensure adequate supply and management of ride tubes, specifically single-rider tubes, to prevent wait frustration

Customer voice summary

Vana Nava Water Jungle receives strong positive feedback for its exceptionally attentive lifeguards, family-friendly environment, and clean facilities. Visitors appreciate the diverse and thrilling slides, low weekday crowds, and good value via hotel perks or ticket discounts. Notable areas of criticism include pests in dining areas, a lack of Halal food choices, slow service at the swim-up bar, and rough wave pool flooring. Recent reviewers report high satisfaction, focusing strongly on the convenient online booking experience and good pricing. Visitors note that while the park is not gigantic, it offers a relaxing and fun atmosphere with short queue times and adequate facilities. Service reliability and overall value stand out as key strengths.

Top positive themes

  • Attentive lifeguards and high safety focus
  • Fun and thrilling slide variety
  • Clean facilities and short wait times
  • Easy and convenient booking process
  • Good pricing and deals
  • Short queue times

Top negative themes

  • Pigeons in dining area
  • Lack of Halal food options
  • Wave pool floor erosion

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Provide certified Halal food options to capture dietary-restricted international visitors.
  • Enforce strict dining area pest control to prevent bird and pigeon issues near food zones.
  • Ensure efficient swim-up bar and food service speed to avoid customer frustration with long wait times.
  • Streamline online booking and digital ticketing for maximum convenience
  • Offer competitive pricing and promotional packages to attract value-conscious visitors
