Customer voice summary

Customers overwhelmingly praise Ramayana Water Park for its extensive variety of exciting slides, clean grounds, and exceptionally attentive, multilingual staff. High marks are given for family friendliness, Russian language accessibility, and overall fun. However, minor criticisms involve long walks and staircase climbs between rides, occasional ride downtime due to maintenance or weather, and paid add-ons like lockers and towels. Visitors consistently praise Ramayana Water Park for its thrilling rides, clean water quality, and excellent overall management. Guests report minimal wait times when arriving at opening, allowing for multiple ride turnarounds. The food inside the park is noted as delicious and reasonably priced, making it a highly recommended destination for families.

Top positive themes

  • Thrilling and varied water slides
  • Friendly and attentive staff service
  • Clean facilities and multi-language support (Russian signs)
  • Thrilling waterslides and rides
  • Short wait times and fast queueing
  • Clean water quality and excellent facilities

Top negative themes

  • Ride downtime and queue delays

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Promote all-inclusive ticket options that bundle lockers, towels, and photos to contrast against extra add-on fees.

  • Capitalize on park downtime issues by ensuring transparent ride status communications and reliable weather operational policies.
  • Promote adult-friendly ride options and open access across attractions to cater to adult groups restricted by kids-only zones.
  • Maintain fast line management and high ride throughput during opening hours to match low wait time experiences.

Customer voice summary

Visitors consistently praise Andamanda Phuket for its clean facilities, friendly staff, and impressive range of high-thrill water slides. However, customer experience is frequently impacted by high prices for food and beverages, extra fees for lockers and towels, and a strict ban on outside drinks. Guests also report long wait times caused by high crowd volumes and a shortage of single and double ride tubes. Visitors highly rate Andamanda Phuket for its clean facilities, exciting ride selection, and helpful shuttle transfer staff, especially during bad weather. Families appreciate the range of activities for both kids and adults alongside manageable wait times. However, guest feedback highlights minor drawbacks such as a shortage of single-rider tubes, extra costs for towels, and shower rooms lacking hooks.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting slides and fun variety of rides
  • Clean and well-maintained park environment
  • Friendly, attentive staff and active lifeguards
  • Exciting and unique ride variety
  • Excellent shuttle and transfer service
  • Family-friendly activities and short line wait times

Top negative themes

  • High food and drink prices with poor quality
  • Extra charges for basic amenities like lockers and towels
  • Long wait times and shortage of inflatable tubes
  • Shortage of single rider tubes
  • Extra charges for amenities like towels
  • Dark toilets and lack of shower hooks

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer transparent or all-inclusive ticket options that cover essential amenities like lockers and towels to avoid guest feeling nickel-and-dimed.
  • Ensure sufficient tube availability and efficient line management to prevent ride bottlenecks caused by guests waiting for returned tubes.
  • Promote affordable, high-quality dining options and clear height-requirement policies before booking to better cater to families with young children.
  • Ensure abundant availability of single-rider tubes and ride equipment to minimize guest bottlenecking.
  • Offer complimentary or transparently bundled amenity options (e.g., towels) to improve value perception.
