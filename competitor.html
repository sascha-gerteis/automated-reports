Customer voice summary

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is praised for its fun and thrilling water rides, family-friendly environment, and attentive staff. However, the overall customer experience is significantly marred by overwhelmingly high prices for everything within the park, exacerbated by a strict no-outside-food-or-water policy. Guests also express frustration over the physical exertion required to carry tubes up slides and raise concerns about maintenance and, critically, water quality. Customers generally praise Columbia Pictures Aquaverse for its wide variety of attractions, including water slides, live shows, and tube rides, making it a great destination for the whole family. The booking process is noted as easy and convenient, and the pizza receives positive feedback. However, there are conflicting views on value, with one customer finding the ticket price great while another felt it was not entirely worth the cost and suggested it might be more suitable for older adults.

  • Perceived value

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Review and adjust overall pricing strategy for tickets, food, and amenities.
  • Re-evaluate outside food and beverage policy, possibly offering free drinking water or more affordable options.
  • Implement solutions for carrying tubes up slides to improve guest comfort and experience.
  • Address maintenance concerns and conduct thorough checks on water quality.
  • Explore options to diversify activities or provide more value for the entry price.

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket receives high praise for its fun, thrilling, and well-maintained water slides, along with the friendly and safety-focused staff. Many visitors enjoy the family-friendly atmosphere and the generally low wait times, especially during off-peak periods. However, a significant drawback for guests is the perception of being overly 'money-focused,' with numerous additional charges for basic amenities like lockers and towels, strict prohibitions on outside food and drinks, and high prices for consumables inside the park. Operational issues like insufficient tubes for popular rides and concerns over sustainability policies also contribute to negative experiences. Based on a single 5-star review, Andamanda Phuket is praised for being very fun and having good staff service. The reviewer expressed an intention to bring children back again, highlighting its appeal for families.

Top positive themes

  • Fun and exciting slides
  • Cleanliness and maintenance
  • Friendly and attentive staff/lifeguards
  • Low wait times (off-peak/early)
  • Suitable for families
  • Fun
  • Good Staff Service
  • Family-friendly

Top negative themes

  • High additional costs (lockers, towels, shoes)
  • No outside food/drinks & overpriced F&B
  • Lack of inflatable tubes for slides
  • Questionable sustainability practices (water policy)
  • Isolated incidents of poor staff service/theft

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer transparent ticket pricing that includes essential amenities (e.g., lockers, towels)
  • Provide more flexible and reasonably priced food and beverage options, potentially including free water refill stations or allowing outside snacks
  • Ensure ample availability of ride equipment (e.g., inflatable tubes) to minimize wait times
  • Implement clear and authentic sustainability initiatives that genuinely benefit guests and the environment
  • Prioritize excellent staff service
