Customer voice summary

Ramayana Water Park receives high praise for its diverse and exciting slides suitable for all ages, friendly and helpful staff, and well-maintained, clean, and green atmosphere. Guests frequently commend the affordable food and lack of crowds. However, some visitors find ticket prices expensive, especially for families, and have reported issues like unexpected attraction closures without notification or inadequate staff follow-up after an incident. Based on a single review, Ramayana Water Park is highly recommended, with a customer praising its spacious venue and clean water quality. This feedback contributes to an average rating of 5 out of 5.

Top positive themes

  • Diverse & exciting rides/slides
  • Friendly & helpful staff
  • Cleanliness & well-maintained park
  • Great atmosphere & green spaces
  • Affordable food options
  • spacious venue
  • clean water quality

Top negative themes

  • Ticket prices (expensive, difference onsite)
  • Unexpected attraction closures (no notification)
  • Safety/maintenance issues (broken step)
  • Lack of staff follow-up post-incident
  • Payment system inconvenience (multiple bracelets)

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Ensure transparent and timely communication for all attraction closures
  • Offer competitive pricing and clear value propositions, especially for families
  • Implement robust staff training for incident response and guest assistance follow-through
  • Maintain exceptional safety and facility conditions, regularly inspecting all infrastructure
  • Provide convenient payment solutions, such as linking multiple family wristbands to a single bill

Customer voice summary

Vana Nava Water Jungle is praised for its fun and exciting rides, catering to both thrill-seekers and families with dedicated children's areas, and noted for its cleanliness and spaciousness. During off-peak times, guests enjoy low wait times. However, a significant drawback is the high cost, with guests feeling it's overpriced due to extra fees for basic amenities like lockers and towels. Furthermore, the overall service is criticized for staff's English communication skills and inattentiveness, and food options are viewed negatively due to restrictions on outside food and closed stalls. Customers give Vana Nava Water Jungle excellent ratings, praising the cleanliness and well-maintained slides. They had a fun time with the open rides and noted the good price. The only negative feedback was the closure of the lazy river during one visit.
