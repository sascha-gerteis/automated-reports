Customer voice summary

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse generally receives praise for its fun and thrilling water slides, themed atmosphere, and professional staff, with many visitors enjoying the separate zones for kids and adults. However, a significant recurring negative theme is the high pricing for entry, food, drinks, and rentals, alongside frustration over carrying heavy tubes up many stairs. Some guests also noted issues with water quality, a boring lazy river, and unfavorable comparisons to a nearby competitor. Despite these challenges, the park offers a variety of entertainment that can be enjoyed all day. Customers consistently rate Columbia Pictures Aquaverse with 5 stars, highlighting the extremely fun and thrilling water slides, excellent rides, and stunning shows. The park offers a great variety of attractions including interactive games and a lazy river, making it a wonderful and family-friendly place for all ages. Booking is noted as easy, convenient, and offering good ticket prices, with low queuing on weekdays. However, guests note that outside food and water are strictly not allowed, and the cost of food and water inside is considered steep, with advice to bring your own towels.

Customer voice summary

Ramayana Water Park is highly praised for its thrilling slides, expansive beautiful grounds, and exceptional, friendly staff, making it an excellent destination for families. However, recurring criticisms highlight the high costs of food and additional services like lockers and towels. Guests also report issues with long queues, occasional ride shutdowns, chilly water, and short operating hours. Customers expressed strong dissatisfaction with the park's operational policy, specifically an early closure after only three hours due to light rain. This resulted in a poor customer experience and negative perception of value for money.

Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket is praised for its fantastic and varied rides, overall cleanliness, and well-maintained facilities, with many guests enjoying the exciting attractions and generally friendly staff. However, several visitors express frustration over the high overall cost, including expensive food, drinks, and additional fees for amenities like lockers. Other notable issues include long wait times for popular slides, a perceived lack of tubes, and concerning reports of inappropriate staff behavior, particularly from a security guard.

Top positive themes

  • Exciting and varied rides
  • Cleanliness and well-maintained park
  • Friendly and helpful staff
  • Large and beautifully decorated park
  • Good for families and children

Top negative themes

  • High cost and hidden fees (food, lockers, towels)
  • Long wait times and insufficient tubes
  • Inappropriate staff behavior
  • Ride closures and inconsistent availability
  • Restrictive height policies for children/pregnant women

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Offer transparent and competitive pricing, including amenities and F&B, potentially allowing outside water or refill stations.
  • Optimize ride operations and queue management to minimize wait times and ensure sufficient equipment.
  • Implement comprehensive staff training on professional conduct, customer empathy, and consistent safety communications.
  • Clearly communicate and consistently apply height restrictions, ensuring a positive experience for all guests, including families with young children or pregnant women.
