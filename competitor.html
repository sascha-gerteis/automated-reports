Customer voice summary

Andamanda Phuket receives high praise for its family-friendly environment, extensive safety measures with numerous lifeguards, and overall seamless experience. Many customers appreciate the good ticket prices found through platforms like Klook. However, a significant negative point involves issues with direct app purchases, where advertised add-ons are reportedly fake, leading to customers having to pay for them again, and advice to purchase tickets locally for better value. Extra charges for locker and towel rentals are also noted. Based on a single, highly positive review, Andamanda Phuket is praised for its fun atmosphere and good staff service. The reviewer expressed a clear intent to return with children, highlighting a positive family-friendly experience.

  • Family-friendly

  • Fun atmosphere

Top negative themes

  • Extra rental costs

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Ensure complete transparency and delivery of all advertised add-ons and packages, especially for app/online purchases.
  • Clearly communicate all potential extra costs, such as locker and towel rentals, upfront.
  • Highlight your waterpark's commitment to safety and abundant staff presence, as this is a strong competitor positive.
  • Emphasize ease of booking and seamless entry across all purchase channels.
  • Ensure excellent staff service to match the competitor's perceived strength

Customers highly praise Vana Nava Water Jungle for its exciting rides, fun atmosphere, and suitability for families. Many appreciate its cleanliness and spaciousness, noting minimal queues during off-peak periods. However, some guests experienced longer wait times for rides during busier periods and expressed mixed opinions on food quality, particularly the buffet, with some also noting closed food stalls. The single review for Vana Nava Water Jungle is overwhelmingly positive, highlighting an excellent experience. Customers appreciate the ease of booking, high-quality facilities, and its appeal to both children and adults.
