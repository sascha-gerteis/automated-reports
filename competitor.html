Customer Voice Summary

Customers generally praise Columbia Pictures Aquaverse for its fun and exciting rides, which cater to all ages, and the helpfulness of many staff members. While general park cleanliness is appreciated, recurring criticisms highlight the high prices for food, drinks, and cabanas, and a strict outside food/water policy that concerns guests. There are also significant, though less frequent, complaints about water quality and the condition of shower facilities, alongside inconsistent staff service at certain counters.

Top positive themes

  • Variety of rides and attractions
  • Fun and exciting experience
  • Friendly and helpful staff (general park/instructors)
  • Cleanliness (general park areas)
  • Online ticket deals

Top negative themes

  • Expensive food and drinks
  • Poor water quality/cleanliness (specific incidents)
  • Substandard/unclean shower and restroom facilities
  • Rude staff at specific counters (cafe, ticket, towel rental)
  • No free drinking water/strict outside food policy
  • Expensive and poor quality cabanas

Implications for Aquaverse

  • Address water quality concerns and improve cleanliness/amenities in shower and restroom facilities.
  • Re-evaluate pricing for food, drinks, and cabanas, and reconsider the strict outside food/drink policy to ensure guest hydration and better value.
  • Implement comprehensive customer service training, especially for front-line staff at F&B, ticket, and towel rental counters.
  • Enhance infrastructure by improving slippery paving areas and providing better parking options and shade.

Customer Voice Summary

Customers consistently rate Andamanda Phuket with 5 stars, highlighting the fantastic variety of rides suitable for all age groups and the overall fun experience. Positive feedback frequently mentions the park's cleanliness, well-maintained facilities, and excellent organization. Short wait times for attractions are a significant advantage, contributing to a great family-friendly visit and offering good value for money on tickets. Andamanda Phuket is praised for its "World Class Water Slides," "Friendly Staff," and overall "Clean" environment. However, significant negative feedback indicates that the park is perceived as "Overpriced." Guests are also dissatisfied with policies prohibiting outside mineral water and children's comfort items, and report that a large percentage of attractions for small children are out of order, directly impacting the family experience.

  • Highlight competitive pricing for tickets, especially for online purchases.
  • Showcase the variety of attractions catering to all age groups, reinforcing family appeal.
  • Ensure pricing offers clear value to customers.
